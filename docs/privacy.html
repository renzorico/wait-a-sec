Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Wait a Sec does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What we store

Wait a Sec stores the following data locally on your device using your browser's built-in storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • Site rules — the domains you choose to block and their delay duration.
  • Usage stats — how many times you attempted, proceeded, or skipped each site. These numbers are only visible to you in the extension popup and settings page.
  • Preferences — whether the extension is globally enabled.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or any third-party services.
  • We do not read the content of the pages you visit — only the URL, to check if a rule applies.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize your data in any way.

Permissions

The extension requests the following browser permissions:

  • webNavigation — to detect when you navigate to a site and check if a rule applies.
  • storage — to save your rules and stats locally on your device.
  • tabs — to communicate between the background script and the page overlay.
  • host permissions (<all_urls>) — to inject the blur overlay on matched sites. The extension only acts on URLs that match your configured rules.

Data deletion

All stored data is deleted when you uninstall the extension. You can also clear it manually by removing all rules in the settings page, or by clearing your browser's extension storage.
